Dataset

Scenes are assembled in NVIDIA Isaac Sim from an industrial asset library (racks, conveyors, CNC machines, pallets, robots, …), sampled with scene-level and object-centric camera strategies, and rendered to RGB, depth, surface normals, and semantic ground truth. Single-view point clouds are back-projected from depth in the camera frame (120k points per frame).

Depth-corruption test sets

Three corrupted variants of the test split (mild / medium / strong) model increasing depth-sensor noise on top of the clean renders; surface normals are re-estimated from the corrupted depth.
